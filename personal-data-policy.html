Personal Data Processing Policy

1. General Provisions

This personal data processing policy (hereinafter — the "Policy") defines the procedure and conditions for processing personal data undertaken by the Teron Linkz team (hereinafter — the "Operator"), and establishes measures to ensure the security of this data in accordance with EU regulations (GDPR).

1.1. The Operator considers the observance of human and civil rights and freedoms in the processing of personal data, including the protection of rights to privacy and professional secrecy, as the most important goal and condition for its activities.

1.2. This Policy applies to all information that the Operator may receive about visitors (hereinafter — "Users") of the website teron-linkz.org.

2. Key Definitions

  • Website — a collection of graphic and informational materials available on the Internet at the address teron-linkz.org.
  • User — any visitor to the website.
  • Personal Data — any information relating to a specific User of the consulting blog.

3. Data We Process

  • Provided by the User: Last name, first name; email address; phone number.
  • Collected Automatically: Anonymized visitor data (cookies) via Google Analytics and similar tools available in Europe.

4. Purposes of Processing

  • Providing access to the platform's expert materials.
  • Establishing feedback for providing free consultations.
  • Sending notifications about new growth strategies and innovations.
  • Opting out of mailings is possible by sending an email to support@teron-linkz.org marked "Unsubscribe from notifications."

5. Legal Grounds

The Operator processes data only with the voluntary consent of the User, expressed through filling out forms on the website, in accordance with the legislation of France.

6. Duration and Security

The period of personal data processing is unlimited. The User can withdraw consent at any time by writing to the Operator's email address. We guarantee data protection against unauthorized access by third parties.
