Privacy Policy

The Privacy Policy applies to personal data provided by clients of Teron Linkz both for the provision of consulting services and for access to educational blog materials in electronic format.

Teron Linkz reserves the right to make changes to the provisions of this Policy. When using external links to third-party resources (e.g., analytics tools), our company is not responsible for their internal privacy policies. By providing your data, you agree to its processing by the methods described below.

Data Collection and Disposal Procedures

We collect data exclusively for personalizing services, providing access to innovative tools, and ensuring expert support. A full complex of technical security measures is used to protect information.

In accordance with European standards, data is stored as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes of its collection or until the company ceases its activities. Destruction or anonymization of data is performed upon the user's request.

Categories of Information Collected

  • Technical: IP address, browser type, access time, pages visited.
  • Personal: User name, e-mail, and phone number (for consultation appointments).
  • Activity: Information about requests sent to the company and selected development strategies.

Purposes of Processing:

  • Providing expert consultations and consulting services.
  • Informing about promotions and special offers in EU countries.
  • Analyzing audience interests for the development of new IT tools.

Transfer to Third Parties

Teron Linkz does not provide personal data of clients to third parties, except in cases of direct user consent or official requirements of French law enforcement agencies.

Your Rights

You have the right to edit, restrict processing, or completely delete your data. To exercise these rights, contact us via the form on the website or via official email: support@teron-linkz.org.
