Cookie Policy

Welcome to Teron Linkz! To improve your experience interacting with our platform, ensure its correct operation, and analyze user activity within the European region, we use cookies and similar technologies. This policy explains in detail which technologies we use, for what purposes, and how you can manage them.

What are cookies?

A cookie is a small piece of data (text file) that a website saves on your device (computer, tablet, or smartphone) when you visit it. This allows the platform to "remember" your actions and preferences (such as language, access region, and other parameters) over a period of time, so you don't have to re-enter them every time you move between pages.

What types of cookies do we use?

We classify the cookies used on the teron-linkz.org website into the following categories:

  • Strictly Necessary Cookies: These cookies are critical for the functioning of the website infrastructure. They allow you to navigate through sections and use basic features, such as accessing protected blog materials.
  • Analytical and Performance Cookies: These cookies collect anonymous information about how visitors interact with our consulting materials. They help us understand which pages are most in demand in France and other EU countries.
  • Functional Cookies: These cookies allow the site to remember your choices (such as interface settings) to provide a more personalized expert experience.
  • Advertising (Marketing) Cookies: These cookies are used to deliver offers that most closely match your professional interests.

Why do we use cookies?

The main purposes of using cookies at Teron Linkz are:

  • Ensuring stable and secure platform operation.
  • Analyzing user behavior to optimize the functionality and structure of the consulting blog.
  • Personalization of innovative content and settings.
  • Providing relevant growth strategies and marketing materials.

Your choice and how to manage cookies

You have full control over cookies. You can change your settings at any time via our pop-up banner or in your browser. Most modern browsers allow you to view, delete, and block cookies. However, please note: disabling strictly necessary cookies may lead to the incorrect operation of some functions of our site.
